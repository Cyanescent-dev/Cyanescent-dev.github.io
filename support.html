Installing safely

Download Cyanescent Player only from this website. Every release is signed with a Developer ID and notarised by Apple, so it opens normally. Check the download against the SHA-256 checksum on the download section if you want to verify it:

shasum -a 256 ~/Downloads/Cyanescent-Player-Free-1.0.0-macOS-universal.zip

If macOS reports that a download is damaged, from an unidentified developer or unverified, stop and download it again from this site. Don’t disable Gatekeeper or remove quarantine to open it.

Common problems

  • Visuals stall or stutter: let the first shader load finish, switch to Standard quality and close other GPU-heavy applications.
  • Live input is silent: check the selected Audio Source, device connection and gain. In System Settings → Privacy & Security, review Microphone access for external input, or Screen & System Audio Recording access for native System Audio capture (macOS 14.2 or later).
  • Missing music: reconnect the drive that holds your files, or use the recovery controls in Music Playlists to locate them again.
  • Pocket Scion not responding: use a data-capable USB cable and confirm the device appears in Audio MIDI Setup, then choose Options → Audio Source → Scion Mode.

The Quick Start covers these in more detail, including how to uninstall an edition without losing shared playlists.

Reporting a problem

Bug reports are welcome on the Cyanescent Player issue tracker on GitHub:

Open the issue tracker

Please include your edition (Free or Pro), the version and build shown in the application menu → About, your macOS version and Mac model, and what you did before the problem appeared.

Issue reports are public. Never post passwords, purchase receipts, private keys, or log and playlist files that contain personal folder paths.

Purchases and receipts

Pro purchases, receipts and the download email are handled by the checkout provider shown at purchase. Its purchase, refund and cancellation terms are shown before you pay and don’t reduce your statutory rights.

Earlier releases

Cyanescent Player 1.0.0 supersedes the 0.9 Preview and 0.9 Fungal Edition. Those previews contain known bugs and older preset implementations, and are no longer offered. Free 1.0.0 is the recommended replacement.
