Kick + bass

Make the foundation of a psytrance track

Psy-Roller brings kick and bass creation together in one VST3 plugin, so you can shape the rhythmic low end in your DAW.

Version 1.0 for macOS

This universal VST3 build includes arm64 and x86_64 support for Apple silicon and Intel Macs. It is notarized by Apple.
