Privacy

Applies to Cyanescent Player 1.0.0 and this website.

Cyanescent Player

  • No account and no licence check. The Player has no sign-in and doesn’t contact a licensing or activation server.
  • No analytics. The Player doesn’t include analytics, advertising or telemetry.
  • Your music stays where it is. Playlists store references to your files, along with your settings, on your Mac in ~/Library/Application Support/Cyanescent Player/. Music and exports aren’t uploaded anywhere.
  • Audio input is analysed on your Mac. Microphone, interface and System Audio capture are used only to drive the visuals, and only after you grant macOS permission.
  • Fonts. When online, the Player requests two typefaces (Exo 2 and Michroma) from Google Fonts. As with any web request, Google receives your IP address and basic request details. See the third-party notices.
  • Links you choose to open. The website link in the About window opens this site in your browser only when you click it.

This website

This site is a static website hosted by GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking scripts. GitHub, which hosts the site and its downloads, and Google Fonts, which serves the site’s typefaces, process standard request information such as your IP address under their own privacy policies.

Purchases

Pro and Pro Video are sold through Lemon Squeezy, which acts as the seller of record. Lemon Squeezy processes the payment, billing and email details you enter at checkout to take payment, calculate tax, and deliver your download and receipt. Its own privacy policy applies to that information. Cyanescent never receives your card details. Buying Pro doesn’t create an account in Cyanescent Player, and the Player doesn’t contact Lemon Squeezy.
